# Quick-Start Prompts

## General

```text
Use procurement-skill.md as the parent skill. Identify the relevant child skill, state assumptions and missing information, follow the skill workflow, and provide a procurement-ready output with risks, next steps, and human review items.
```

## Intake

```text
Use procurement-intake-skill.md to review this request. Separate the business need from the proposed solution, identify missing information and risks, prepare stakeholder questions, and present possible next steps for human decision.
```

## Market research

```text
Use market-research-skill.md. Define the market boundary, identify supplier types, build an evidence-based longlist, compare capabilities, review pricing models and risks, and flag items requiring validation.
```

## RFx drafting

```text
Use rfp-drafting-skill.md to draft this section. Identify missing inputs, separate mandatory, rated, pricing, and contractual requirements, draft supplier-facing language, challenge ambiguity, and provide human review items.
```

## Pricing review

```text
Use pricing-model-review-skill.md. Do not merely summarize the pricing. Identify cost components, cost drivers, future exposure, comparability issues, clarification questions, and human decisions.
```

## SLA review

```text
Use sla-review-skill.md. Test every SLA against metric, measurement, reporting, remedy, and escalation. Identify weaknesses, missing KPIs, business-owner questions, and required reviews.
```

## Contract review

```text
Use contract-risk-review-skill.md. Explain material clauses in plain language, identify commercial and operational impact, prioritize risks, prepare negotiation points, and separate legal escalations.
```

## Negotiation preparation

```text
Use negotiation-prep-skill.md. Build a fact-based negotiation brief with objectives, leverage, prioritized asks, reciprocal concessions, supplier pushback, fallback positions, and approval items.
```

## Supplier QBR

```text
Use supplier-qbr-skill.md. Compare performance to contractual commitments, identify trends and overdue actions, review commercial and renewal risks, prepare supplier questions, and build a focused agenda.
```

## Spend analysis

```text
Use spend-analysis-skill.md. Inspect data quality, preserve the source data, normalize suppliers, classify spend, identify overlaps, anomalies, contract gaps, renewal risks, and opportunities requiring validation.
```
