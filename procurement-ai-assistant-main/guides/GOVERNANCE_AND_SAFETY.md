# Governance and Safety

## Data handling

- Use only AI tools approved by your organization.
- Remove confidential, personal, supplier-proprietary, evaluation, and commercially sensitive information unless the tool is approved for it.
- Do not upload credentials, API keys, passwords, or security secrets.
- Follow records-management and retention requirements.

## Human accountability

AI may support:

- Research
- Summarization
- Drafting
- Comparison
- Risk identification
- Scenario preparation
- Quality checks

Humans must own:

- Procurement strategy
- Policy interpretation
- Evaluation scoring
- Supplier selection
- Award recommendation
- Legal approval
- Negotiation mandate
- Contract signature
- Risk acceptance

## Public procurement

Where public procurement rules apply, protect:

- Fairness
- Transparency
- Equal treatment
- Objective requirements
- Consistent evaluation
- Confidentiality
- Conflict-of-interest controls
- Audit defensibility

## Validation

Before relying on an output:

- Confirm facts against source documents
- Verify citations and supplier claims
- Review assumptions and missing information
- Obtain specialist review
- Preserve an audit trail where required

## No autonomous evaluation

The skill library must not be used to autonomously score bids, select suppliers, or make award decisions.
